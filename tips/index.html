Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. What should I do if a student cannot log in?

Check the student’s AILA ID in the Students tab. If needed, click Reset Password to generate a temporary password and share it securely.

Q2. How can I see which students are not engaging with the platform?

Open the Analytics Dashboard. The section No Engagement Students highlights students who haven’t logged in recently.

Q3. We have more students than available seats. What’s the solution?

Go to the Seat Keys tab to check available seats. If all are used, request additional licenses from your AILA account manager.

Q4. Can I reassign a seat key from one student to another?

Yes. Once a key expires or is released, it can be reassigned to another student based on your school’s policy.

Q5. How do I know if students are studying consistently?

Check the Streak Distribution graph in the Analytics Dashboard. It shows how many consecutive days students are active.

Q6. What if a student forgets to copy the temporary password during reset?

You will need to click Generate Password again. Once closed, the password cannot be retrieved.

Q7. How can I contact support if I face technical issues?

Go to the Support tab and click Send Email, or email directly at support@aila.sa. Always include student ID or seat key details when relevant.

Teaching Tips & Best Practices

Review Analytics Weekly

Check the Analytics Dashboard at least once a week to monitor student activity and identify early warning signs.

Encourage Streaks

Motivate students to maintain daily study streaks. Regular sessions improve long-term retention.

Reassign Seats Promptly

If a student leaves, reassign their seat key to maximize usage.

Use Filters for Efficiency

Use filters (grade, status, exam) to quickly locate information.

Reset Passwords Responsibly

Share new temporary passwords securely and remind students to update them.

Celebrate Progress

Acknowledge and celebrate students’ achievements to boost motivation.

Set Clear Goals for Students

Help students set achievable goals and track progress regularly to maintain focus and motivation.

Share Insights with Your Team

Share observations with colleagues to align strategies and improve outcomes.
